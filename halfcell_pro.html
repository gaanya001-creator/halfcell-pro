📍 Reference Electrode Design — Ender 2017 FEM study
Ender 2017 key finding: RE position and design critically affects measured half-cell impedance. Point RE creates maximum artifacts. Ring/mesh RE centered between electrodes gives cleanest spectra.
Three-electrode cell geometry — RE position effect on current distribution (Ender 2017 Fig. 3)
❌ Point RE (worst)
Artifact: Z_artifact = f(r_RE, κ, ω)
Radial current → distorted Nyquist. Frequency-dependent loops appear. Both geometric + electrochemical asymmetry cause issues. Avoid!
✓ Ring RE (better)
Geometric artifact reduced by ring geometry
Ring RE averages radial current distribution. Electrochemical asymmetry still present but geometric artifact suppressed. Used in EL-CELL PAT setup.
✓✓ Mesh RE (best)
Both artifacts minimized
Mesh RE between electrodes eliminates radial current. Recommended by Ender 2017. Use with glass fiber separator for further improvement. Costard 2017 (Part II) experimentally confirmed.

📐 All Governing Equations
1. Charge conservation — solid (Ohm's law)
∇·(−σ∇φ_s) = −a·j_p (0<x<L_p)
2. Charge conservation — electrolyte
∇·(−κ∇φ_e) = a·j (−L_s<x<L_p)
3. Mass conservation — Li in particle (Fick 2nd, spherical)
∂c/∂t = (1/r²)·∂/∂r(D·r²·∂c/∂r) (0<r<R_p)
4. Boundary conditions — particle
∂c/∂r|₀=0 (symmetry) | ∂c/∂r|_R=−j_p/(F·D) (flux BC)
5. Butler-Volmer — PyBaMM sinh form
j_p = 2·j₀(c_surf)·sinh(F·η/2RT) | η=φ_s−φ_e−U(c_surf)
6. Exchange current j₀ (NMC)
j₀ = k·c_e^0.5·c_surf^0.5·(c_max−c_surf)^0.5 | k=6×10⁻⁷
7. SEI growth (Ecker/Marquis model)
dδ/dt = j_SEI·M_SEI/(2F·ρ_SEI) | ρ=1690 kg/m³, M=73 g/mol
8. EIS — Warburg (solid diffusion)
Z_W(ω) = σ·ω^(−0.5)·(1−j) | σ=RT/(n²F²A·C·√(2D))
9. DRT — Tikhonov regularization (2024-25)
Z(ω) = R∞ + ∫g(τ)/(1+jωτ)·d(lnτ) | λ=0.15 regularization
10. Ender 2017 — geometric artifact
Z_artifact ∝ (r_WE−r_CE)²/(κ·L_sep) [radial current distortion]
11. Kramers-Kronig validation
Z_KK(ω) = R∞ + Σ Rk/(1+jωτk) | residual <1% → valid EIS
12. Randles-Ševčík (CV)
i_p = 0.4463·n·F·A·C·√(n·F·D·ν/RT)
